---
title: Basque Burnt Cheesecake (Tarta de Queso)
tags: ["spanish", "dessert", "cake", "cheesecake"]
---

Inspired by La Viña in San Sebastián, this crustless cheesecake is baked at high heat for a deeply caramelized, blistered top and a custardy, nearly molten center. Makes one 20 cm (8-inch) cake (6–8 servings).

### Ingredients

- Cream cheese (full-fat, room temperature): 600 g
- Granulated sugar: 200 g
- Eggs (large, room temperature): 4
- Heavy cream (35% fat, room temperature): 300 ml
- All-purpose flour (sifted): 25 g
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp (optional)
- Fine salt: 1/2 tsp

### Method

1. Line a 20 cm (8-inch) springform pan with two overlapping sheets of parchment paper, pressing them into the corners and leaving at least 5 cm of overhang above the rim (the cake rises dramatically in the oven). Crumpling the parchment first makes it easier to fit into the pan.
1. In a large bowl or stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the room-temperature cream cheese and sugar on medium-low speed for 2–3 minutes until completely smooth and lump-free, scraping down the sides of the bowl. Avoid whipping too much air into the batter.
1. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low speed until each is just incorporated before adding the next.
1. Pour in the heavy cream, vanilla extract (if using), and salt, and mix on low speed until combined.
1. Sift the flour directly over the batter and fold gently until completely smooth and no streaks remain.
1. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and tap it gently on the counter a couple of times to pop any large air bubbles.
1. <BakingStep temp="220" time="30–35 minutes" fan preheat /> (or 240°C static) until the top is deeply caramelized mahogany-brown (almost burnt in spots) and the cake has puffed up, while the center still jiggles noticeably like a loose custard when gently nudged.
1. Remove from the oven and let cool completely in the pan at room temperature (it will sink and form its signature rustic folds as it cools).
1. Serve at room temperature for a very soft, spoonable center, or chill in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours (or overnight) for cleaner, creamier slices. Bring chilled slices back toward room temperature for 15–20 minutes before serving.
