Classic French brown-butter and almond mini cakes with crisp, caramelized edges and a tender, nutty crumb—ideal alongside a cappuccino. Makes 12–15 financiers (using rectangular financier molds or a mini muffin pan).

### Ingredients

- Unsalted butter: 150 g (plus extra for greasing the molds)
- Almond flour: 100 g
- Powdered sugar (icing sugar): 160 g
- All-purpose flour: 60 g
- Fine salt: 1/4 tsp
- Egg whites (room temperature, ~4 large eggs): 150 g
- Honey (mild, e.g. acacia): 15 g (1 tbsp)
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- Flaked almonds: 2 tbsp (optional, for topping)

### Method

1. **Make the beurre noisette (brown butter):** Melt the butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling occasionally, until it foams, crackles, and develops golden-brown milk solids with a toasted-hazelnut aroma (5–7 minutes). Immediately pour into a heatproof bowl (including the browned bits, or strain if you prefer a cleaner crumb) and let cool until warm, around 45–50°C.
1. **Optional flavor boost:** Lightly toast the almond flour in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant and pale golden. Let cool completely.
1. In a large bowl, sift together the powdered sugar, all-purpose flour, almond flour, and salt.
1. Add the egg whites, honey, and vanilla extract to the dry ingredients. Whisk gently until smooth and just combined—do not whip air into the whites.
1. Pour the warm brown butter into the batter in two additions, stirring with a whisk or spatula until glossy and homogeneous.
1. Cover the batter and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (or overnight). Resting hydrates the flour and gives the financiers crisp edges and a slight dome in the oven.
1. Generously butter your financier molds (or mini muffin tin) and place them on a baking sheet.
1. Fill each cavity about 3/4 full (a piping bag makes this clean and easy) and scatter a few flaked almonds on top, if using.
1. <BakingStep temp="190" time="12–15 minutes" fan preheat /> until the edges are deeply golden-brown and the centers spring back lightly when pressed.
1. Let rest in the molds for 3–5 minutes, then unmold onto a wire rack to cool. Enjoy slightly warm or at room temperature with a cappuccino.

### Variations

- **Hazelnut (noisette):** Replace half or all of the almond flour with roasted hazelnut flour for an even deeper pairing with coffee.
- **Citrus:** Rub the zest of 1/2 lemon or orange into the dry ingredients before adding the egg whites.
- **Berry:** Press 1–2 fresh raspberries into each cavity right before baking.
