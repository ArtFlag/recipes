title: Baked Pumpkin Spice Donuts

*Makes:* Approximately 8 donuts  
*Prep time:* 15 minutes  
*Bake time:* 12–16 minutes

## Ingredients

**For the donuts**

- Weizenmehl Type 550: 250 g
- Unsweetened pumpkin purée: 365 g
- Eggs (large): 2
- Brown sugar: 110 g
- White sugar: 75 g
- Olive oil: 80 ml
- Butter (melted): 57 g
- Backpulver: 8 g
- Pumpkin spice: 2 tsp
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- Salt: 3 g

**For the cinnamon-sugar coating**

- Butter (melted): 40 g
- Granulated sugar: 50 g
- Ground cinnamon: 1 tsp

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the oven and pan:** Preheat the oven to **175°C Ober-/Unterhitze or 160°C Umluft**. Grease your donut pan thoroughly with butter or baking spray.
1. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk together until smooth and well combined:
   - Pumpkin purée
   - Eggs
   - Brown sugar
   - White sugar
   - Olive oil
   - Melted butter
   - Vanilla extract
1. **Mix the dry ingredients:** In a separate bowl, combine:
   - Flour
   - Backpulver
   - Pumpkin spice
   - Salt
1. **Combine:** Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients. Fold together gently until just combined. Don't overmix, or the donuts may become dense.
1. **Fill the donut pan:** Transfer the batter to a piping bag or a sturdy freezer bag with the corner snipped off. Pipe the batter into the donut cavities, filling each approximately **¾ full**.
1. **Bake:** <BakingStep temp="175" time="12–16 minutes" preheat /> (or 160°C fan), until the donuts spring back when lightly pressed and a toothpick inserted into the thickest part comes out clean. Let them cool in the pan for approximately 5 minutes before carefully removing them.
1. **Add the cinnamon-sugar coating:** Mix the granulated sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. While the donuts are still slightly warm:
   1. Brush each donut with melted butter.
   1. Roll it gently in the cinnamon-sugar mixture.
   1. Place it on a wire rack to finish cooling.

   For a less-sweet result, apply a light coating rather than covering every surface heavily.

## Notes

- **Type 550 flour works 1:1** in this recipe; you don't need to adjust the liquid.
- Use plain, unsweetened pumpkin purée, not sweetened pumpkin pie filling.
- If your purée is particularly watery, drain off excess liquid.
- These are baked cake-style donuts, not fried donuts.
- The reduced sugar amounts above are the version we chose for a less-sweet batter.
