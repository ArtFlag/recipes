---
title: Brown Butter & White Miso Cake
tags: ["japanese", "dessert", "cake", "miso"]
---

A tender, deeply aromatic butter cake where nutty browned butter and sweet white miso (shiro miso) combine into a rich, butterscotch-like umami flavor, finished with a glossy miso caramel glaze. Makes one 20 cm (8-inch) round cake or one 23 × 10 cm loaf (8–10 servings).

### Ingredients

**For the cake:**
- Unsalted butter: 150 g
- White miso paste (shiro miso): 40 g (~2 tbsp)
- Light brown sugar (packed): 150 g
- Granulated sugar: 50 g
- Eggs (large, room temperature): 3
- Sour cream (or full-fat Greek yogurt, room temperature): 120 g
- Whole milk (room temperature): 60 ml
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- All-purpose flour: 210 g
- Baking powder: 1.5 tsp
- Baking soda: 1/4 tsp
- Fine salt: 1/4 tsp

**For the miso caramel glaze:**
- Granulated sugar: 100 g
- Water: 2 tbsp
- Heavy cream (35% fat, warmed): 80 ml
- Unsalted butter: 30 g
- White miso paste (shiro miso): 15 g (~1 tbsp)
- Toasted sesame seeds (white or black): 1 tbsp (optional garnish)

### Method

#### 1. Cake

1. Grease and line a 20 cm (8-inch) round cake pan (or a 23 × 10 cm loaf pan) with parchment paper.
1. **Brown the butter:** Melt the butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling occasionally, until it foams, crackles, and develops deep golden-brown milk solids with a toasted-hazelnut aroma (about 5–7 minutes). Immediately pour into a large mixing bowl (including all the browned bits) and let cool for 10 minutes until warm, not hot.
1. Whisk the white miso paste into the warm brown butter until completely dissolved and smooth.
1. Add the brown sugar and granulated sugar and whisk vigorously until well combined.
1. Whisk in the eggs one at a time, then whisk in the sour cream, milk, and vanilla extract until smooth.
1. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
1. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture with a spatula just until no streaks of flour remain—do not overmix.
1. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
1. <BakingStep temp="175" time="40–45 minutes" preheat /> (50–55 minutes if using a loaf pan) until deeply golden on top and a skewer inserted into the center comes out clean.
1. Let cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

#### 2. Miso Caramel Glaze

1. Combine the granulated sugar and water in a small heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat. Swirl gently until the sugar dissolves, then boil without stirring until the syrup turns a rich amber color.
1. Remove from the heat and carefully pour in the warmed heavy cream (it will bubble vigorously), whisking until smooth.
1. Whisk in the butter and white miso paste until completely melted, glossy, and emulsified. If any tiny miso specks remain, pass the glaze through a fine-mesh sieve.
1. Let the caramel cool for 10–15 minutes until slightly thickened, then pour over the cooled cake and scatter with toasted sesame seeds (if using).
