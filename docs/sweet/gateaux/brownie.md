## Classic Brownies

19x19cm square pan

- 200g dark chocolate
- 110g (1/2cup) unsalted butter
- 3 eggs
- 130g (3/4cup) granulated sugar
- 50g (1/4cup) dark brown sugar
- 65g (1/2cup) all-purpose flour
- 25g (1/4cup) cocoa powder(unsweetened)
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

1. Melt chocolate and butter over water bath.
1. In another bowl, mix sugar, vanilla extract and eggs.
1. Set over water bath to disolve the sugar.
1. Away from the heat, beat until white and fluffy. If you don't beat the eggs enough, it'll look like fudge.
1. Add melted chocolate.
1. In a different bowl, mix salt, flour and cocoa powder.
1. Add it to the batter through a sifter.
1. Mix delicately.
1. <BakingStep temp="170" time="20-30 minutes" preheat />

## Brown Butter Miso Brownies

Deeply fudgy, intensely chocolatey brownies with chewy edges, a glossy crinkly top, and a subtle savory-sweet umami depth from browned butter and white miso (shiro miso). Makes one 20 × 20 cm (8-inch) square pan (9–16 brownies).

### Ingredients

- Unsalted butter: 150 g
- White miso paste (shiro miso): 30 g (~2 tbsp)
- Dark chocolate (70% cocoa, finely chopped): 200 g
- Unsweetened Dutch-processed cocoa powder: 30 g
- Instant espresso powder: 1/2 tsp (optional)
- Eggs (large, room temperature): 3
- Granulated sugar: 140 g
- Dark brown sugar (packed): 80 g
- Vanilla extract: 1 tsp
- All-purpose flour: 80 g
- Dark chocolate chunks (or chopped dark chocolate): 60 g (optional, for melty pockets)
- Flaky sea salt: for finishing (optional)

### Method

1. Line a 20 × 20 cm (8-inch) square baking pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on two sides for easy lifting.
1. **Brown the butter:** Melt the butter in a light-colored saucepan over medium heat, swirling occasionally, until it foams, crackles, and develops golden-brown milk solids with a nutty aroma (about 5–7 minutes).
1. Remove from the heat immediately and whisk in the white miso paste until completely smooth and emulsified.
1. Add the finely chopped dark chocolate, cocoa powder, and espresso powder (if using) to the warm brown butter-miso mixture. Let sit for 1 minute, then stir gently until completely melted and glossy. Set aside to cool slightly until warm, not hot.
1. **Whip the eggs and sugars:** In a large bowl (or stand mixer with the whisk attachment), beat the eggs, granulated sugar, dark brown sugar, and vanilla extract on medium-high speed for 4–5 minutes until pale, thick, and ribbony—this dissolves the sugar and creates the glossy, crinkly brownie top.
1. Pour the warm chocolate-miso mixture into the whipped eggs and fold gently with a rubber spatula until just combined.
1. Sift the flour directly over the batter and fold gently until only a few streaks remain, then fold in the optional dark chocolate chunks until no dry flour is visible. Do not overmix.
1. Scrape the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top evenly into the corners.
1. <BakingStep temp="175" time="25–30 minutes" preheat /> until the edges are set, the top is glossy and crackled, and a skewer inserted into the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs (not wet batter).
1. Sprinkle lightly with flaky sea salt (if using) while still warm.
1. Let cool completely in the pan on a wire rack (at least 1.5–2 hours) before lifting out and slicing. For extra-clean, ultra-fudgy squares, chill in the fridge for 30 minutes before cutting with a warm, wiped knife.

### Notes

- **Miso choice:** Use sweet white miso (*shiro miso*), not red or dark miso, which can overpower the chocolate. Because miso already provides salt, no extra fine salt is needed in the batter.
- **Storage:** Keeps in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 4 days, or in the fridge for up to a week (the fudgy texture gets even better on day two).
