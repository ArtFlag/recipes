## Apple cider reduction

- Apple cider: 4 cups
- Cinnamon stick: 1
- Salt: 1 pinch

1. Add the apple cider, cinnamon stick, and pinch of salt to a saucepan.
1. Bring to an easy boil over medium heat.
1. Simmer gently until reduced down to 1 cup.
1. Remove from heat and let cool before using.

## Cocktail

- Rye whiskey: 2 oz (60 ml)
- Sweet vermouth: 3/4 oz (22.5 ml)
- Apple cider syrup: 1/4 oz (7.5 ml)
- Angostura bitters: 1 dash
- Orange bitters: 1 dash
- Ice: as needed

1. Add rye whiskey, sweet vermouth, apple cider syrup, Angostura bitters, and orange bitters to a mixing glass.
1. Add ice and stir until thoroughly chilled (about 30 seconds).
1. Strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass.
1. (Optional) Garnish with a fresh apple slice or brandied cherry.
