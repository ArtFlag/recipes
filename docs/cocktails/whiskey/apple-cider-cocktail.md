## Apple cider reduction

- Apple cider: 4 cups
- Cinnamon stick: 1
- Salt: 1 pinch

1. Add the apple cider, cinnamon stick, and pinch of salt to a saucepan.
1. Bring to an easy boil over medium heat.
1. Simmer gently until reduced down to 1 cup.
1. Remove from heat and let cool before using.

## Cocktail

- Bourbon: 60 ml (2 oz)
- Reduced apple cider: 15 ml (0.5 oz)
- Angostura bitters: 1 dash
- Ice: as needed

1. Add the bourbon, reduced apple cider, and Angostura bitters into a mixing glass.
1. Add ice.
1. Stir until well chilled.
1. Strain into a rocks glass with ice.
