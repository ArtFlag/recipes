## Ingredients

- Eggplant (Chinese or Italian): 300g (1 large or 2 medium)
- Potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet): 300g (2 medium)
- Green bell pepper: 150g (1 large)
- Garlic: 5 cloves
- Scallion: 1 stalk
- Ginger: 1 small slice (5g)
- Light soy sauce: 2 tbsp (30ml)
- Dark soy sauce: 1 tsp (5ml)
- Sugar: 1 tbsp (12g)
- Chinese black vinegar (Chinkiang) or Shaoxing wine: 1 tsp (5ml)
- Cornstarch (for sauce): 1 tbsp (10g)
- Cornstarch (for coating eggplant): 2 tbsp (20g)
- Water (or vegetable stock): 1/2 cup (120ml)
- Salt (for eggplant and seasoning): 1 tsp
- White pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Cooking oil (for shallow-frying): 1/2 cup (120ml)

## Method

1. Cut the potatoes into bite-sized wedges using the roll-cut technique (cut at an angle, roll the potato a quarter turn, and cut again). Rinse in cold water to remove excess surface starch, then pat completely dry with paper towels.
1. Roll-cut the eggplant into similar bite-sized chunks. Place them in a bowl, sprinkle with 1/2 tsp of salt, and let sit for 10 minutes to draw out excess moisture. Squeeze out excess liquid gently with paper towels, then toss the eggplant evenly with 2 tbsp of cornstarch. This forms a barrier so the eggplant absorbs much less oil when frying.
1. Deseed the green bell pepper and cut or tear it into bite-sized pieces.
1. Finely mince the garlic (divide into two halves: one for cooking, one for the finishing touch). Thinly slice the scallion and finely mince the ginger.
1. In a small bowl, prepare the sauce by whisking together the water, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, vinegar (or Shaoxing wine), white pepper, remaining 1/2 tsp of salt, and 1 tbsp of cornstarch until smooth.
1. Heat about 1/2 cup of oil in a wok or deep skillet over medium heat (around 175°C / 350°F). Add the potato pieces in a single layer and shallow-fry, turning occasionally, for 5 to 7 minutes until tender on the inside and golden-crisp on the outside. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
1. Increase heat to medium-high. Add the cornstarch-coated eggplant chunks to the hot oil and fry for 2 to 3 minutes until lightly browned and soft. Remove and drain alongside the potatoes.
1. Add the green bell pepper pieces to the hot oil for 20 to 30 seconds until slightly blistered but still vibrant green and crisp. Drain with the other vegetables.
1. Pour off nearly all the oil from the wok, leaving about 1 tbsp.
1. Over medium heat, add half of the minced garlic, the scallion, and ginger to the wok. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
1. Whisk the sauce mixture once more to ensure the starch has not settled, then pour it into the wok. Stir continuously until the sauce bubbles, turns translucent, and thickens into a glossy glaze.
1. Return the fried potatoes, eggplant, and green pepper to the wok. Toss vigorously to coat every piece in the sauce.
1. Turn off the heat, add the remaining half of the minced raw garlic, and give everything one final toss. Serve immediately with hot steamed rice.
