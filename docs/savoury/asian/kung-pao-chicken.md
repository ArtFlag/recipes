title: Kung Pao Chicken

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Marinade

- Chicken thighs (boneless, skinless): 450g
- Light soy sauce: 1 tbsp (15ml)
- Shaoxing wine: 1 tbsp (15ml)
- Cornstarch: 1 tbsp (10g)
- Water: 1 tbsp (15ml)
- Salt: 1/4 tsp

### Kung Pao Sauce

- Sugar: 1.5 tbsp (20g)
- Chinkiang black vinegar: 1.5 tbsp (22ml)
- Light soy sauce: 1.5 tbsp (22ml)
- Dark soy sauce: 1/2 tsp (2.5ml)
- Shaoxing wine: 1 tbsp (15ml)
- Chicken stock (or water): 2 tbsp (30ml)
- Cornstarch: 1 tsp (3g)

### Stir-Fry & Aromatics

- Roasted unsalted peanuts: 75g (1/2 cup)
- Dried red chilies: 10-15 pieces
- Sichuan peppercorns: 1 tsp
- Garlic: 3 cloves
- Ginger: 1 thumb-sized piece (15g)
- Scallions (white and light green parts): 3 stalks
- Neutral cooking oil: 2.5 tbsp (37ml)

## Method

1. Cut the chicken thighs into 1.5 cm (1/2 inch) bite-sized cubes.
1. In a medium bowl, combine the diced chicken with 1 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tbsp water, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1 tbsp cornstarch. Massage until the liquid is absorbed and the chicken has a velvety coating. Let marinate for 15 to 20 minutes.
1. In a small bowl, prepare the sauce by whisking together the sugar, Chinkiang black vinegar, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, chicken stock, and 1 tsp cornstarch until the sugar is fully dissolved.
1. Snip the dried red chilies in half and shake out the loose seeds. Thinly slice the garlic and ginger. Cut the scallions into 1.5 cm segments (matching the size of the chicken).
1. Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and sear for 2 minutes without moving, then stir-fry for another 1 to 2 minutes until just cooked through. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.
1. Add the remaining 1.5 tbsp of oil to the wok and reduce heat to low. Add the dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns. Fry gently for 30 to 45 seconds until fragrant and darkened, taking care not to scorch them.
1. Add the sliced garlic, sliced ginger, and the white segments of the scallions. Stir-fry over medium heat for about 30 seconds until aromatic.
1. Return the cooked chicken and any resting juices back to the wok. Increase the heat to high and toss everything together for 30 seconds.
1. Whisk the sauce once more to redistribute the cornstarch, then pour it into the wok. Stir-fry vigorously for 1 minute until the sauce bubbles and forms a rich, glossy coating over every piece of chicken.
1. Toss in the roasted peanuts and remaining green scallion pieces. Stir for 15 seconds to incorporate and turn off the heat immediately so the peanuts remain crisp.
1. Transfer to a serving dish and serve hot with steamed white rice.
