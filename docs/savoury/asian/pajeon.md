---
title: Pajeon (Korean Scallion Pancake)
tags: ["korean", "pancake", "appetizer", "vegetarian"]
---

Makes 1 large pancake (2 to 3 servings).

## Pancake

- Scallions (green onions): 180 g (1 large bunch, trimmed)
- All-purpose flour: 100 g (3/4 cup)
- Potato starch or cornstarch: 30 g (1/4 cup)
- Ice-cold water: 160 ml (2/3 cup)
- Salt: 1/2 tsp
- Garlic powder (or 1 clove finely grated): 1/2 tsp
- Egg: 1 (lightly beaten, optional)
- Red or green chili: 1 (thinly sliced, optional)
- Neutral cooking oil (canola, sunflower, or vegetable): 3–4 tbsp (45–60 ml)

## Dipping Sauce (Cho-ganjang)

- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp (30 ml)
- Rice vinegar: 1 tbsp (15 ml)
- Water: 1 tbsp (15 ml)
- Sugar: 1/2 tsp
- Toasted sesame oil: 1/2 tsp
- Toasted sesame seeds: 1 tsp
- Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes): 1/2 tsp (optional)

## Instructions

1. Make the dipping sauce by whisking together the soy sauce, rice vinegar, water, sugar, toasted sesame oil, sesame seeds, and gochugaru in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves. Set aside.
1. Wash and thoroughly dry the scallions. Cut them into lengths that fit neatly across a 24–26 cm (10-inch) skillet, or into 5–7 cm pieces. Split any thick white stems lengthwise so they cook evenly.
1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, potato starch (or cornstarch), salt, and garlic powder. Pour in the ice-cold water and whisk lightly until just combined—a few small lumps are fine, and the batter should be slightly thinner than crepe batter.
1. Heat 2 tbsp of neutral oil in a large nonstick or well-seasoned skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
1. Dip the scallions in the batter to coat lightly, then arrange them in a single, even layer in the hot skillet. Pour about two-thirds of the remaining batter evenly over the scallions to bind them together into a flat pancake (do not drown them; the scallions should remain the star).
1. Scatter the sliced chili over the top if using. Drizzle the lightly beaten egg over the surface, followed by any remaining batter to fill in gaps.
1. Cook undisturbed for 3 to 4 minutes, pressing down gently with a spatula, until the bottom is deeply golden brown and crisp and the edges look set.
1. Flip the pancake carefully. Drizzle the remaining 1–2 tbsp of oil around the edges of the pan so it runs underneath, press down gently with a spatula, and cook for another 3 to 4 minutes until crisp and golden on the second side.
1. Transfer to a cutting board, cut into bite-sized squares or wedges, and serve immediately while hot and crisp with the dipping sauce.

:::tip[Tips & Variations]
- **Maximum crispiness**: Ice-cold water and starch keep the batter light and crunchy. Avoid overcrowding with too much batter, and add a touch of extra oil around the rim after flipping.
- **Seafood Pajeon (Haemul Pajeon)**: Scatter 100–150 g of patted-dry, chopped shrimp, squid, or clams over the scallions before drizzling the egg and remaining batter.
- **Kimchi variation**: Mix chopped, well-drained [kimchi](../fermented/kimchi.md) into the batter along with 1–2 tbsp of kimchi brine (reducing the water slightly) for a kimchi-scallion pancake.
:::
