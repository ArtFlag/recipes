---
title: Stuffed Peppers with Ground Turkey
tags: ["american", "peppers", "turkey", "main", "stuffing"]
---

- Bell peppers: 4 (large)
- Ground turkey: 450 g (1 lb)
- Rice: 200 g
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Yellow onion: 1 (medium, diced)
- Garlic: 3 cloves (minced)
- Diced tomatoes: 400 g (1 can, drained)
- Tomato sauce: 240 ml (1 cup)
- Italian seasoning (blend of dried oregano, basil, rosemary, and thyme): 1 tsp
- Smoked paprika: 1/2 tsp
- Ground cumin: 1/2 tsp
- Salt: 1 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/2 tsp
- Shredded cheese (cheddar or Monterey Jack): 100 g (1 cup)
- Fresh parsley: 2 tbsp (chopped)
- Water: 60 ml (1/4 cup)

1. Cut off the tops of the bell peppers, discard the seeds and white membranes, and trim a tiny sliver off the bottoms if necessary so they stand upright.
1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
1. Add the ground turkey to the skillet. Season with Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, cumin, salt, and black pepper. Cook, breaking up the meat with a spoon, until browned and cooked through, about 6–8 minutes.
1. Stir in the drained diced tomatoes, cooked rice, and half of the tomato sauce (120 ml / 1/2 cup). Simmer on low heat for 2–3 minutes to let the flavours meld. Remove from the heat and fold in half of the shredded cheese (50 g) and the chopped parsley.
1. Pour the water and the remaining half of the tomato sauce into the bottom of a baking dish. Place the peppers upright in the dish.
1. Spoon the turkey and rice filling generously into each pepper cavity, pressing down gently.
1. Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake: <BakingStep temp="190" time="30-35 minutes" preheat /> until the peppers are tender.
1. Remove the foil, scatter the remaining shredded cheese over the top of each pepper, and bake uncovered for another 5–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling.
1. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving, garnished with extra fresh parsley if desired.

:::tip[Preventing Soggy Peppers]
- **Pre-roast the empty peppers**: Pre-bake the hollowed-out peppers cut-side down in the oven for 10–15 minutes before stuffing to release excess moisture.
- **Drain wet ingredients**: Thoroughly drain canned tomatoes and let the filling simmer until any excess liquid evaporates before spooning it into the peppers.
- **Salt and invert**: Lightly salting the inside of raw peppers and letting them rest upside down on paper towels for 10 minutes pulls out surface water.
- Rice must be al dente.
:::
